# Proactive Campaigns

Rather than waiting for visitors to initiate a chat, **Campaigns** pop up targeted conversational teasers and promotional bubbles based on visitor behavior, current URL path, and referral source.

Proactive nudges increase website conversion rates by up to **3.2x** by presenting relevant answers at high-intent decision moments.

---

## Trigger Rules & Targeting

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Time Delay" icon="clock">
    Displays the teaser message after the visitor has spent <i>N</i> seconds on the page (e.g. 15 seconds on a pricing comparison table).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Scroll Depth" icon="file">
    Triggers when the visitor scrolls down a specific percentage of the page (e.g. 50% down a case study or feature breakdown).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Page Path Filtering" icon="link">
    Target or exclude specific URLs using glob patterns (e.g. `/pricing*`, `/checkout/*`, `!/docs/*`).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Frequency Caps" icon="repeat">
    Configurable display frequencies: <b>Once per session</b>, <b>Once per user</b>, or <b>Every page visit</b>.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Managing Campaigns in Dashboard

1. Navigate to the **Campaigns** tab in the Chatty Dashboard.
2. Click **Create Campaign** and select your target bot.
3. Configure the **Teaser Headline**, **Call-to-Action Text**, and **Trigger Conditions**.
4. Activate the campaign to start displaying teasers on your live site immediately.
